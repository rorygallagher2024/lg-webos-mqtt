What LG is allowed to collect
These are the agreements recorded on the TV. Off is the private setting.
These cannot be changed from here — the TV owns them. Change them on the TV itself under Settings → General → About This TV → User Agreements, then reopen this panel to confirm.
Background services
Whether LG's data-collection services are currently running. A service can be running while its permission is switched off — it simply has nothing it is allowed to send.
On-TV ad & telemetry sinkhole
Sinkholes known LG telemetry, ad servers, and Automatic Content Recognition (ACR) tracking endpoints directly on the TV by bind-mounting a local blackhole table over /etc/hosts.
Two of the blocked domains are LG infrastructure rather than pure ad hosts: ngfts.lge.com (content and firmware delivery) and lgtvsdp.com (the service platform behind the LG Content Store). With this on, firmware updates and the app store may stop working. That is the trade — turn it off if you need either.
LG Ad & Analytics Blackhole
Blocks ad.lgsmartad.com, ibis.lgappstv.com, rdx.lgtvcommon.com, and 12 other tracking endpoints.
